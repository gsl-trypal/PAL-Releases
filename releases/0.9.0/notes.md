# PAL 0.9.0

The first beta of PAL. Thank you for trying it this early. Some things will be rough: when one is, Settings › Help › Send feedback writes to the people who make PAL.

- [box] PAL runs on a box in your home. Your files, your voice and what you ask stay there.
- [android] The Android app pairs with your PAL at home, and reaches it when you are out.
- [mac] The Mac app pairs with your PAL and brings it to your desktop: dictation, Ask PAL and meeting notes.
- [android] [mac] Settings › About shows which version of the app and of your PAL you have, and these notes.
- [android] [mac] After an update, "What's new" shows once with what changed.
